Pieces of Forever

The little moments that quietly became my favorite memories.

01

The First Picture

The first time we met was on December 25th.

Few months before that, I had randomly messaged a girl on Instagram asking for a follow-for-follow. Little did I know that, months later, that same girl would show up in front of me on her white Activa.

And somehow, that girl went from being a random Instagram connection to someone who would make a permanent place in my heart.

Here’s one of the first photos from the beautiful journey we started together.

02

A Trip to Remember

My second-ever trip with my crush — and honestly, I can’t even explain how much I enjoyed it.

I’m the kind of person who is perfectly happy with just a few close friends. I don’t usually feel comfortable around completely new people. But that trip was different. I went with a bunch of people I barely knew, simply because I wanted to spend more time with her.

And somewhere along the way, I clicked my favourite photo of her.

She absolutely hates that photo… but bhaiiii, look at her! She looks so damn cute in it, doesn’t she? ❤️

Looking back, I think the best part of that trip wasn’t the place, the people, or even the journey — it was getting to spend those moments with her.

03

The Day We Met Again

We had a fight once, and after that, we completely stopped texting each other.

But somehow, some kind of miracle happened — thanks, Piyush bhai — and we slowly started talking again. At first, it was just a little here and there, but eventually, things started feeling normal again.

Then she went on a trip, and when she came back, she came to Delhi before leaving for Jaipur and called me to meet her.

Honestly, I didn’t want to go at first. I was scared and kept wondering, “Why am I doing this?” But you know how it is… dil nahi maanta na.

So I went.

That day, I brought her flowers for the first time. And man… she looked so beautiful. For a moment, it honestly felt like I was dreaming.

We only met for about 30–40 minutes, and we didn’t really get to spend all that time together because she was with her friends. But even those few minutes meant a lot to me.

And of course, we clicked a picture together.

I’ll be honest — I looked terrible in that picture 😂, but she looked gorgeous as always.

Maybe that’s what made the picture special anyway.
