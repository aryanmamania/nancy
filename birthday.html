if we're still together...

My Love,

If you're reading this while we're still together, then there's one thing I want you to know first — I'm really glad it's still us.

Somewhere between all the random conversations, silly moments, little arguments, laughter, and memories, you became someone incredibly important to me.

I don't want to take that for granted. I don't want to assume that just because you're here today, you'll always know how much you mean to me.

So let me say it clearly: I choose you. And I hope I keep choosing you, again and again.

I hope we continue making memories that we'll laugh about years from now. I hope we continue annoying each other, supporting each other, celebrating each other's wins and being there through the difficult days too.

I don't expect everything between us to always be perfect. Real relationships aren't perfect. But I hope we're always honest enough to talk, strong enough to stay, and kind enough to understand each other.

And if one day you look back at this little birthday website, I hope you remember how much love was behind it.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for being part of my life. And thank you for giving me so many reasons to smile.

If we're still together, then I hope this isn't the end of our story. I hope it's just another beautiful chapter.

Happy Birthday, beautiful. Here's to us, to everything we've already lived, and to everything that's still waiting for us.

Always yours ♡
With all my heart

if life took us separate ways...

Dear You,

If you're reading this because we're no longer together, then I know this letter probably feels different from the one you might have expected to find here.

But I still wanted to leave these words for you because what we had mattered. Whatever happened between us, whatever changed, and wherever life takes us from here — our memories were real.

Some people stay forever. Some people become memories. And sometimes, a person can be important even when they aren't part of your life anymore.

I don't want to remember us only for the ending. I want to remember the laughter, the silly conversations, the little moments, the photographs, the days when everything felt right, and all the things we once dreamed about.

If I ever hurt you, I'm sorry. If there were things I should have understood sooner, I'm sorry for those too. We were both learning, growing, and trying to figure things out.

I don't know what the future looks like for either of us now. Maybe our paths will cross again. Maybe they won't. And I think it's okay not to know.

What I do know is that I genuinely hope life is kind to you. I hope you laugh a lot. I hope you find people who appreciate your heart. I hope you achieve the things you once told me about.

And wherever you are, I hope you're happy. Truly happy.

So if this birthday finds you reading these words, please remember one thing: you were loved. You are worthy of love. And you will always have been an important chapter of my life.

Thank you for the memories. Thank you for the lessons. Thank you for being part of my story.

Happy Birthday, Akanksha. I hope the life ahead of you is beautiful.

Take care, always ♡
With nothing but good wishes
